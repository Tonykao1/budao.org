## 2. Homepage theology — quote the canonical Chinese; do not reinvent it

The actual homepage currently says:

- **我们在做什么？** — “健行于天地，相知于彼此，在旷野经历与祂的同在。”
- **为什么是‘步道’？** — “祂就是道路、真理、生命。唯有祂亲自感动你同行天路，共步主道。”
- **‘步道’有什么？** — “一个拥抱、一条线路、一则故事、一段经文、一起祷告” (the final item is an interactive prayer invitation).

The existing faith declaration is **“余生行走，不偏左右”**. Its owner-approved English version is held in the translation charter and homepage preview. Do not use this document to change the approved translation or source design.

These lines, rather than a new speculative doctrinal manifesto, are the authorized **homepage-level** expression of BUDAO's faith foundation and practice. Any more detailed statement of faith, ecclesiology or doctrine requires separate review and approval.

## 3. What is really present in 同道 today?

**Verified source:** `tongdao-data.js` contains historical packs with structured fields such as `theme`, `storyTitle`, `icebreakers`, `scriptureReference`, `scriptureText`, `topics.topic1` through `topic7`, and `growthChallenge`.

**Verified source:** `tongdao-app.js` implements an interactive facilitation flow with stages for introducing a pack, group preparation, welcome, icebreakers, warmup, prayer, group walk, story, Scripture, response, blessing, closing prayer, and follow-up growth challenges. This is *existing prototype/practice material*, not automatically an endorsed manual for every cultural or church setting.

For internationalization, extract an **editorial, read-only outline** of a typical walk as explanatory material first. Do not wire the live interactive app into English or claim a complete internationally approved teaching course until actual pedagogy, safeguarding, rights, translations and field testing are reviewed.

Do not reproduce full copyrighted picture books, third-party audio/video or licensed Bible translations without permission. Keep proper book identification, original-language context and review of permissible short quotations.

## 4. Next-stage leader-formation videos — PLANNED

**Owner-stated future direction:** BUDAO will develop training videos to equip leaders.

Required design decisions *before production*:
- Audience and prerequisites: local churches, existing leaders, prospective leaders, individual learners.
- Content outline drawn from verified BUDAO practice, not guessed instructional rules.
- Leadership, participant consent, safeguarding, emergency preparation and responsible pastoral boundaries.
- How leaders are recognized and supported locally; watching a video does **not** automatically grant site-publishing or leading permissions.
- Language review, transcript/caption production and accessibility.
- Editorial and theological review and real-life pilot assessment.

No video count, episode length, publication date, credential, enrollment URL, or certification is approved by this note. Never present courses as available before they exist.

## 5. 52-week devotional discipleship plan — PLANNED

**Owner-stated future direction:** a **52-week spiritual formation / devotional discipleship plan**.

The plan may in future connect personal Scripture engagement to weekly practice and to real community and church life, but this is a design hypothesis, **not an approved 52-week syllabus**.

Before writing the weeks, decide the canonical Scripture framework, doctrinal review, progression, reflection and spiritual practice methods, group/pastoral safeguarding, copyrighted Bible-translation permissions, target cultures, and whether/how it works alongside the leader videos.

Do not invent 52 already completed weeks, claim the program is available, or confuse it with a separate “52 picture books” editorial series.

## 6. International content sequence — suggested, not automatic production approval

**Stage A — source inventory and editorial mapping:** maintain homepage as authoritative mission copy; inventory the historical 同道 packs; map which steps are descriptive, optional, safety-critical or locally adaptable.

**Stage B — read-only introductory training outline:** owner reviews a short explanation of the existing method; present historical examples with accurate citations/rights review. No edits to the current 同道 experience before a separate product decision.

**Stage C — video and 52-week course architecture:** create outlines and learning objectives for review, then pilot with real leaders and local churches. Do not use placeholder enrollment links, “available now” CTAs, certification claims or unreviewed spiritual prescriptions.

**Stage D — international release:** publish approved content in appropriately reviewed languages. Ensure mission, participation, registration, privacy, minors and safety materials are accurate in each target locale.
